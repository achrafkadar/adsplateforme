Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wenov (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the Wenov Ads Platform (the “Service”), including surfaces used for Meta App Review.

Who we are

Wenov operates an agency advertising platform used to manage client campaigns and related messaging workflows. Contact: privacy@wenov.ca.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as email address used to authenticate operators.
  • Usage data such as pages visited within the Service and basic diagnostic logs.
  • Platform connection data when linking advertising or messaging accounts (for example Meta), limited to what is required to provide the Service.

How we use information

  • To authenticate users and secure the Service
  • To operate client management, campaign oversight, and messaging features
  • To comply with legal obligations and platform policies (including Meta)

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with infrastructure providers that host the Service, and with advertising platforms when you authorize a connection, solely to provide requested functionality.

Data retention

We retain account and operational data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law.

Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission over the Internet is fully secure.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting privacy@wenov.ca.

Meta / Facebook data

When Meta permissions are granted, we process Page, messaging, and ads-related data only to deliver features demonstrated for App Review and client operations. Users may revoke access via Meta settings. To request deletion of Meta-related data held by Wenov, email privacy@wenov.ca with subject “Meta data deletion”.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will be revised when changes are posted.
